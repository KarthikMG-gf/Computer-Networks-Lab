TASK 6 - MAC ADDRESS NOTES

Address: 01:00:5e:0a:01:02
Classification: Ethernet multicast/group address.
Reason: 01:00:5e is the IPv4 multicast mapping prefix.

Address: 3c:52:82:4e:11:07
OUI: 3C-52-82
Meaning: The first three bytes identify the organization assigned the MAC
address block by the IEEE Registration Authority. Use the current IEEE OUI
registry to determine the registered organization name.
