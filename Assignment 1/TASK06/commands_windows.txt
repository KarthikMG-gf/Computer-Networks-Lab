TASK 6 - WINDOWS ETHERNET SETTINGS

1. Open Device Manager.
2. Expand Network adapters.
3. Right-click the active Ethernet adapter and select Properties.
4. Open the Advanced tab.
5. Select Speed & Duplex.
6. Choose Auto Negotiation (or the matching setting supported by both ends).
7. Click OK.
8. Disable/enable the adapter if required, then verify the link status.

Take screenshots of the adapter setting before and after the change.
The switch port must be configured consistently.
