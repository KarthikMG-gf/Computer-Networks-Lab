TASK 12 - PRIVATE 5G AND 50 AGVs

A. Impact analysis
The assignment does not specify the packet size or transmission interval for
each AGV. Do not invent these values. Obtain them from the instructor or
clearly state an engineering assumption before calculating added AGV traffic.

If each AGV sends S bytes every T seconds:
Traffic per AGV (bps) = (S * 8) / T
Traffic for 50 AGVs (bps) = 50 * S * 8 / T
